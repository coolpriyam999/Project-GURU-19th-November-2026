19 NOVEMBER

Dear Naina Ma'am,

Happy Birthday!

I wanted to do something a little different this year, because a simple birthday message didn't quite feel enough.

I've genuinely enjoyed being your student. I'll remember the classes, the conversations after the lessons, helping you pack up, and all those little moments that made tuition something I looked forward to. Even the times you scolded me have somehow become part of the memories I smile about.

More than anything, thank you for being approachable, kind, and encouraging. You have been much more than a teacher to me. you are someone I respect as a Guru.

I hope this new year of your life brings you happiness, good health, peace, and countless reasons to smile. Thank you for everything, Ma'am.

With respect and gratitude,

Lazyfisher
